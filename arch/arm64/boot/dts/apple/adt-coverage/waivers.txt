# board-glob ADT-path-glob | reason
# Patterns apply to a single path component; ** is deliberately unsupported.
# Hardware without a DT representation must remain MISSING until reviewed.
* /arm-io | ADT SoC bus container; child hardware is inventoried separately.
* /cpus | CPU collection container; individual CPUs are checked separately.
* /arm-io/pmgr/power-domains | ADT power-domain collection container; individual domains require review.
* /arm-io/pmgr/power-devices | ADT power-device collection container; individual power-state records are checked separately.
